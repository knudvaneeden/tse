BULLET portable package version 1.0.0.0.1
Updated by OpenAI Codex

Compile INI.S, BULLET.S, and optionally AUTOWRAP.S in this directory.
Keep the resulting INI.MAC, BULLET.MAC, and AUTOWRAP.MAC together.
Load BULLET.MAC, not INI.MAC.

The original 1995 precompiled macros are stored in ORIGINAL_MAC and do not
contain the portable path changes.
