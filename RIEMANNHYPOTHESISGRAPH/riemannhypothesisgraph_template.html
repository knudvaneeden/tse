Mathematical note: The trivial zeros are at negative even integers. The critical line has real part 1/2; zeros found on it are displayed. The Riemann Hypothesis asserts that every non-trivial zero lies on that line; this graph does not establish that assertion. At s=1, zeta has a pole. Heights are clipped for readability, and the surface is sampled/approximated rather than mathematically exhaustive. The main view is the complex image w=zeta(s) of selected straight lines in the input s-plane. All zeros map to w=0, so their distinct locations are shown in the domain-plane view. The domain-plane view contains level curves of Re(zeta) and Im(zeta). Curves are sampled numerical approximations; the 3D view uses magnitude as height.
